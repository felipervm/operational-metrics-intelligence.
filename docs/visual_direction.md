# Visual direction and asset provenance

This is an independent portfolio case, not a Fortra product page. The visual direction follows the company reference's deep forest, mint and Poppins typography, with original concept artwork and live charts built from the fictional dataset.

## Original concept artwork

- Method: built-in ImageGen image-generation tool, new image, opaque background.
- Delivered web asset: [decision-lens.jpg](../site/assets/decision-lens.jpg). The generated master was converted to JPEG for web delivery without changing the composition.
- Role: a decorative metaphor for scattered operational inputs becoming a clear decision lens. It is not measured evidence, a real system or a screenshot of a company product.
- The author portrait was supplied by Felipe Mattos and is separate from the generated artwork.

### Final generation prompt

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: website hero editorial artwork for an independent operational business intelligence portfolio case. Primary request: premium photorealistic CGI image visualizing scattered operational inputs converging into a clear decision lens. Wide 3:2 landscape composition. Dark petrol green-black background (#001b18) with luminous mint/aquamarine accents (#6ce5c1). Translucent layered glass plates and fine optical filaments flow from fragmented depth on the left into one beautifully resolved circular glass lens in the right half. Subtle sophisticated volumetric light and studio reflections, restrained mint edge lighting, high-end contemporary technology art direction, physically convincing optical glass, photographic depth of field. Keep the left third very dark and quiet for optional editorial copy, main sculpture on right. This is abstract concept artwork, not a diagram or quantitative chart. No text, no letters, no numbers, no labels, no logos, no branded objects, no charts, no dashboard interfaces, no shields, no padlocks, no people. Background opaque, polished and elegant rather than neon gaming aesthetics.

## Typography

Poppins Latin WOFF2 files are embedded locally at build time; the page does not request Google Fonts at runtime. The source files came from the official Google Fonts distribution. Their SIL Open Font License is retained in [OFL.txt](../site/assets/fonts/OFL.txt).

## Interaction principles

All values remain live HTML. Severity selection keeps the cohort definition explicit, open records remain separate from resolved-late records, and the executive brief includes the proposed owner and the question to validate. Motion respects reduced-motion preferences and does not animate fabricated improvements.
