Different populations. Different questions. Both calculations can be correct. Neither alone establishes operational health.

Open the metric definitions+

A / Overall closure

Resolved tickets ÷ all created tickets. All severities and all three teams. Completion volume, without a timeliness requirement.

B / Critical cohort completion

Critical tickets resolved within the 72-hour sample target ÷ all created critical tickets. Open tickets remain in the denominator.

Boundary of the exercise

Fictional July–September 2026 intake. Calendar-hour targets omit pauses, reopens and business-hour rules. This is not contractual SLA compliance.
